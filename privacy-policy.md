# Privacy Policy

**Position: Leisure** ("we," "us," "our") provides an AI-powered job search and application tracking platform for technology professionals. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and your rights regarding your data.

**Effective Date**: April 28, 2026
**Last Updated**: April 28, 2026

---

## 1. Who We Are

Position: Leisure is operated by Steven Cantwell, based in Kansas, USA. Following our pending business entity formation, the service will be operated by AlgoAxiom LLC, a Kansas limited liability company. References to "we" in this policy refer to the operator of the service.

For privacy inquiries, contact **algoaxiom.steve@gmail.com**.

## 2. Service Availability

Position: Leisure is currently available only to users located in the United States. We do not knowingly accept account registrations from users in the European Union, United Kingdom, or other non-US jurisdictions. Users under 18 years of age are not permitted to use the service.

## 3. Information We Collect

### 3.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account information**: Email address, password (stored hashed), full name, and (optionally) phone number for two-factor authentication.
- **Resume content**: We accept resumes in PDF, DOC, or DOCX format up to 10 MB. Files are stored in encrypted private storage. Text content is extracted and processed to identify skills, work history, education, and other profile information.
- **Profile information**: Contact details, professional summary, work experience, education, skills, certifications, GitHub or portfolio URLs, side projects, and technology stack per role.
- **Job preferences**: Desired job titles, salary range, preferred locations, remote work preferences, employment type preferences, work authorization status, and sponsorship requirements.
- **Application tracking data**: Jobs you save, dismiss, or apply to; notes you write about applications; tags you create; status updates as you progress through interview stages; cover letter drafts and interview preparation drafts you generate.
- **Saved searches**: Search queries you save and email notification preferences for new matches.

### 3.2 Information We Collect Automatically

- **Authentication tokens**: We issue JSON Web Tokens (JWTs) for authenticated sessions. These are stored in your browser's local storage and the Chrome extension's local storage.
- **Usage telemetry**: Microsoft Application Insights collects anonymized telemetry about how you use the service, including pages visited, features used, error reports, and performance metrics. This helps us improve reliability and user experience.
- **Cookies and similar technologies**: We use functional cookies for authentication. We do not use third-party advertising or behavioral tracking cookies.
- **Email engagement**: SendGrid records whether emails we send are delivered, opened, and clicked. Used to monitor deliverability and prevent abuse.

### 3.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Google OAuth**: If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and Google account ID. We do not access any other Google account data.
- **Job listings (JSearch API)**: When you search for jobs, we query JSearch (operated by RapidAPI) for public job listings from sources including Indeed, ZipRecruiter, Glassdoor, and Google Jobs. Job listing data is publicly available information about employers, not personal information about you.

### 3.4 Information from the Chrome Extension

If you install our Chrome extension, the extension reads job listing information from LinkedIn.com job pages when you click the "Save to Position: Leisure" button. The extension parses job titles, companies, locations, and descriptions from the page content and sends this information to your account. The extension does not read or transmit data from any other websites and does not track your browsing activity.

## 4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Provide the core Position: Leisure service: parsing your resume, matching you with jobs, scoring matches against your profile, generating cover letters and interview preparation drafts, and tracking applications.
- Authenticate you and secure your account, including via email verification and optional two-factor authentication.
- Send transactional emails: account verification, password resets, daily job match digests (if you opt in), saved-search notifications (if you opt in), and important service announcements.
- Improve the service through aggregate usage analysis and error monitoring.
- Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access.
- Comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your information with advertisers or use it for behavioral advertising.

## 5. AI Processing of Your Data

Position: Leisure uses Anthropic's Claude AI to process your resume text and job listing content to provide intelligent matching, cover letters, and interview preparation. When you use these features:

- **What is sent to Anthropic**: Resume text content (after extraction from your PDF/DOC file), job description text, and your profile data when generating personalized output (cover letters, interview prep). The original resume file is not sent — only extracted text.
- **What is not sent**: Your password, payment information, IP address, or browsing activity.
- **Anthropic's data handling**: Per Anthropic's API terms, your data is not used to train Claude models. Anthropic may retain inputs and outputs for up to 30 days for abuse detection purposes. Anthropic's privacy practices are governed by their privacy policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy.
- **AI-generated content disclaimer**: Cover letters, interview prep drafts, match scores, and gap analyses are AI-generated and may contain inaccuracies. They are starting points for your review, not final products. You should always review and edit AI-generated content before submitting it to employers.

## 6. Who We Share Your Information With

We share data with the following service providers, all of whom are bound by their own privacy and security obligations:

| Provider | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---|---|---|
| Microsoft Azure | Hosting, database, file storage, telemetry | All service data (encrypted at rest and in transit) |
| Anthropic | AI processing | Resume text, job descriptions, profile context (see Section 5) |
| RapidAPI / JSearch | Job listing search | Search query terms (no personal data) |
| Google | OAuth authentication (only if you choose Google sign-in) | OAuth tokens and basic profile info you authorize |
| SendGrid (Twilio) | Transactional email delivery | Recipient email address, email content |

We do not share your information for marketing, advertising, or commercial profiling purposes.

We may disclose your information if required by law, valid legal process, or to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others.

## 7. Data Retention

- **Active accounts**: We retain your information for as long as your account is active.
- **Account deletion**: When you delete your account, we permanently delete or anonymize your data within **30 days**. Some information may be retained longer if required by law.
- **Backups**: Encrypted backups may persist for up to 90 days as part of standard infrastructure resilience practices, after which they are overwritten.
- **Telemetry data**: Application Insights data is retained for 90 days.
- **Email engagement data**: SendGrid retains email event data for 30 days.

You may request immediate deletion at any time via your Settings page or by emailing **algoaxiom.steve@gmail.com**.

## 8. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data: Use the "Export my data" feature in Settings to download a complete JSON archive of your information.
- **Correct** inaccurate data: Edit your profile, preferences, and applications directly in the application.
- **Delete** your account and data: Use the "Delete my account" option in Settings, or email us. Deletion is permanent and irreversible.
- **Opt out** of non-essential communications: Toggle daily digest emails and saved-search notifications in your Settings, or use unsubscribe links in any email.
- **Withdraw consent** for AI processing: Delete your account. AI processing is core to the service and cannot be selectively disabled.

To exercise these rights, use the in-app controls or contact **algoaxiom.steve@gmail.com**. We will respond to verifiable requests within 30 days.

## 9. California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act:

- The right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose.
- The right to delete your personal information (subject to certain exceptions).
- The right to opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information).
- The right to non-discrimination for exercising your rights.

To exercise your CCPA rights, follow the procedures in Section 8.

## 10. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data, including:

- HTTPS/TLS encryption for all traffic between you and our servers.
- Encrypted storage at rest for databases and files (Azure-managed encryption).
- Hashed passwords (we never store passwords in plain text).
- JWT authentication with reasonable expiration windows.
- Authentication rate limiting to prevent brute-force attacks.
- Optional two-factor authentication via email.
- Private blob storage for uploaded files (no public URLs).
- Regular security review of dependencies and configurations.

No security system is perfect. If we discover a breach affecting your information, we will notify you in accordance with applicable law.

## 11. Children's Privacy

Position: Leisure is not intended for and may not be used by individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If we discover we have collected information from someone under 18, we will delete it promptly.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via email or in-app notice at least 14 days before they take effect. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy will reflect the most recent revision.

Continued use of the service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
